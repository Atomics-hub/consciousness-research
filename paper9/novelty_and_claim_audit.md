# Hostile Novelty and Claim Audit

## Outcome

**Novelty status: defensible as a methods/null contribution; no field-shifting headline cleared.**

The broad claims that connectomes underconstrain neural dynamics, that task-equivalent networks can have different internal activity, and that perturbations reveal causal structure are established prior art. The narrower delta located here is an executable, topology-preserving, cell-type-block substitution assay that requires source-clamped local equivalence before testing recurrent activity, an unchanged pretrained decoder, and within-system perturbation effects; it also records G0 failures and uses a solver veto and stopped matched-error gate.

## Exact and near collisions

| Prior art | Occupied claim | Remaining delta | Collision verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lappalainen et al., *Nature* 2024 | Connectome-constrained task-optimized fly visual model predicts neural activity | Substitute alternate neuron laws by cell-type block after explicit local matching | Source platform, not same experiment |
| Beiran & Litwin-Kumar, *Nature Neuroscience* 2025 | Same-connectome networks can show dynamical degeneracy; sparse recordings constrain it | Within one public biological model, test whether locally admitted substitutions compose across ordinary/decoder/causal endpoints | Strong conceptual collision; empirical design remains distinct |
| Pospisil et al., *Nature* 2024 | Connectome-informed estimation of a state-dependent effectome | Controlled model substitution with known source and unchanged decoder | Causal motivation occupied; not same assay |
| Network morphism / Net2Net | Function-preserving architectural transforms | No training-based morphism; dynamics-family substitution under fixed connectome and local gate | Broad preservation claim occupied |
| Model stitching and causal abstraction | Functional/causal comparison across representations or models | Cell-type dynamics replacement and recurrent composition in a connectome-constrained neural model | Methodological neighborhood occupied |

No exact prior art was located in the completed 2024–2026 primary-literature scan for the combined design. This is an absence-of-evidence statement, not proof of priority.

## Claim ladder

**Permitted:**

- Local eligibility varied by block and checkpoint.
- All 45 locally eligible ordinary ladder cells preserved block and unchanged-decoder endpoints within frozen margins.
- A Mi1 timescale substitution produced a replicated, solver-robust decoder-effect magnitude discrepancy.
- The matched-error follow-up failed before confirmatory holdout exposure.
- The protocol distinguishes local, ordinary, decoder, and causal equivalence.

**Requires a new confirmatory generation:**

- Causal gain is systematically more sensitive than ordinary behavior.
- A specific dynamics family has privileged causal amplification.
- A robustness frontier generalizes across source checkpoints or cell types.

**Prohibited:**

- Connectomes are sufficient or insufficient in biological brains.
- Locally equivalent neuron dynamics generally compose.
- The experiment models biological neural replacement.
- The work measures consciousness, experience, identity, survival, or preservation.

## Title audit

Selected: **Stress-Testing Local Neural-Dynamics Equivalence in a Connectome-Constrained Visual System**.

Rejected as overstated: “When Local Neural Equivalence Fails to Compose” (ordinary composition failure did not occur); “Causal Organization Survives Substrate Replacement” (substrate and organization claims exceed the evidence); “Connectome Sufficiency Under Neural Replacement” (neither biological sufficiency nor replacement was tested).
