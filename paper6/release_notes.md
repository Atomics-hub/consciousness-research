## Result Boundary

The preregistered full-scaffold versus no-memory gate failed in the straight-condition fixture: full scaffold and no-memory both scored 12/12 on the core continuation score. Full scaffold also did not outperform summary-only. Sham stability passed. The supported claim is therefore narrower than the original Scaffold Continuity Trap thesis: task-local repository artifacts can carry enough causal structure for action-level recovery in small coding-continuation tasks.

The paper does not claim AI consciousness, personhood, moral status, survival, subjective continuity, or internal persistence of a base model.

## Public-Safety Boundary

Coordinator-private scaffolding, hidden keys, condition maps, blind packet archives, runner return packets, and internal Codex workspace outputs are not included in this public release.
