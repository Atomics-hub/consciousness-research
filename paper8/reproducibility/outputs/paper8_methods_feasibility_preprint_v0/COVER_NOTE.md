# General preprint and venue cover note

Dear Editor or Reader,

Please consider *The Binding Test: Construction Audit, Synthetic Planning, and
an Exploratory Feasibility Case Study of Causal Choice-Control Evaluation for
AI Systems* as a methods and evaluation paper.

The paper addresses a measurement problem: observing an AI system choose and
perform an action does not establish that its later output depends on whether
its own earlier choice controlled the assigned history. We introduce a
restricted block-yoked design that randomizes that choice-to-history relation
while matching assigned schedule marginals and retaining every randomized path
in a nominal `L/H/U` endpoint.

The release deliberately separates deterministic construction checks, seeded
synthetic planning, and one exploratory provider feasibility trajectory. The
provider observation revealed that one visible assistant turn may contain more
than one provider-reported model activation, making transport provenance part
of the scientific method rather than a background engineering detail.

The manuscript does not report a randomized provider contrast or causal model-
behavior estimate. It does not make claims about preference, utility, welfare,
consciousness, or subjective experience. Its contribution is a falsifiable
causal measurement architecture, a reproducible construction audit, and an
empirically motivated transport boundary for a future preregistered study.

Thomas Ryan is the sole author and accepts full responsibility for the work.
Generative AI assistance is disclosed on the first page and in the manuscript.
