## Evidence partition

The Stage 1 statement that its release analyzes no provider observation remains
true inside the sealed Stage 1 boundary. This overlay does not rewrite that
historical claim. It adds and separately labels Stage 10 and Stage 11 evidence.

The three evidence layers must never be pooled as though they had the same
inferential status:

- construction checks establish local implementation properties;
- simulations establish conditional operating characteristics; and
- the provider trajectory establishes one workflow instance.

None establishes the randomized enforcement-policy effect.
