# Literature delta search: 2026-08-08 through 2026-08-19

**Search date:** 2026-08-19  
**Status:** submission-date collision check; not a systematic review

## Scope

This delta repeated the exact-conjunction search after the Stage 1 literature
cutoff. Searches targeted arXiv, OpenReview, OSF, and primary project records
for combinations of LLM choice or preference, yoking or matched control,
choice-contingent execution, and held-out/follow-up behavior.

The search was intended to discover a collision with the five-feature
conjunction, not to establish absence across all literature.

## Result

No newly located record combined all of the following:

1. a mechanically scored baseline choice before treatment;
2. prospective assignment to own-choice-contingent versus yoked execution;
3. schedule and affordance marginal matching;
4. execution followed by a held-out mechanically scored choice; and
5. full nominal `L/H/U` reporting with separate observed and partially
   identified binary decisions.

The closest records remain those already documented in the Stage 1 appendix,
especially Zhou and Ackerman on elicited utilities as later incentives, Geng et
al. on passive-intervention yoking, and Blandfort et al. on matched
direction-flipped contextual influence.

The OpenReview title for the Blandfort record is now cited as *Moral Preferences
of LLMs Under Directed Contextual Influence* at
<https://openreview.net/forum?id=PwXopj6hM1>. This update changes citation
metadata, not the collision assessment.
